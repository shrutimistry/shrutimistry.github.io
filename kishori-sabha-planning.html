Creative Ideas & Suggestions

  • Interactive Ghosti Format

    Turn Presentation 2 into a discussion-style ghosti where kishories answer guided questions rather than a one-way presentation. Great for engagement and deeper reflection.

  • Satsang Jeopardy

    Use a Jeopardy-style quiz for exam prep (already planned for Feb 15 & 22). Consider a reusable Canva template so future prep sessions are quick to set up.

  • Monthly Theme Posters

    Kayla can create a themed Canva poster each month that visually ties together both presentation topics, helping kishories see the connection.

  • Presenter Spotlight

    Briefly introduce each presenter at the start of sabha (their seva, how they prepared, or a fun fact). Builds community and encourages others to sign up.

  • Post-Sabha Feedback Form

    Send a quick 2-question Google Form after each sabha: "What was your biggest takeaway?" and "What topic would you like next?" Mahi can share the link during announcements.
